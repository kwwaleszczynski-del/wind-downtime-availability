Wind turbine downtime, its causes and availability

Stops, deratings and curtailment found in 10-minute SCADA data without status codes, classified into IEC 61400-26 categories, priced in lost energy and turned into time- and energy-based availability. Three open wind farms: Kelmarsh (6 x Senvion MM92), Penmanshiel (14 x Senvion MM82) and Hill of Towie (21 x Siemens SWT-2.3-VS-82). Everything below is measured on test periods the methods never saw. Code and method: github.com/kwwaleszczynski-del/wind-downtime-availability.

2. Which detector?

Events are matched when they share at least one 10-minute interval. GBM (Senvion) was trained on Kelmarsh 2016-2019 and Penmanshiel 2018 only; on Hill of Towie it is the transfer to another manufacturer, and GBM (in-farm) the same model trained on Hill of Towie's own first half-year.

3. Availability calculator

Choose a farm, how the categories are treated by the contract, and whose classification feeds it. The numbers are the test periods' totals; the presets are illustrative, the real definitions are in your contract and in IEC 61400-26-1/-2.

Liquidated damages here = max(0, guaranteed - actual time availability) x potential energy x price, one common form; contracts differ (bands, caps, energy-based guarantees).
